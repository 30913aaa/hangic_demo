How to Play

You are a magus. All you hold are sevenOrigin Seeds — 水, 火, 土, 木, 口, 人, 手. Put them on the synthesis table and new characters appear; each is a spell with its own rank and effect. The demo opens 225 researchable formulas; the full version contains 2,892 characters.

1. Take characters apart, then put them back together

The table has four material slots. Place two to four cards and press Synthesize — materials are broken down to their base seeds, then recombined according to the laws.

木＋木＝林 Two 木 make 林
木＋木＋木＝森 Three 木 make 森

A synthesised character becomes a new material. 「森＋森」 equals six 木 — with only four slots, this is the only way to reach the higher ranks.

2. The result is never revealed in advance

Once materials are placed, the display says only “awaiting first observation”.Only pressing Synthesize reveals it. This is deliberate — the pleasure here is not looking things up, but building your own instinct for forging characters.

Combinations you tried without result are recorded; place the same materials again and it will note “no resonance before”. Failure costs nothing, and the materials stay on the table — swap one card and try again.

These two things are all you need to begin. The game starts with a five-step hands-on guide; laws and catalysts are taught when you first encounter them.

Complete laws, catalysts, and collection systems (read later)

3. Eight laws will stop you

Magic cannot be combined at will. When a synthesis breaks a law, a red message appears, telling you which law it was and how to resolve it。

  • R1 Law of Rank Gap　Materials may not differ by more than 7 ranks.
  • R2 Law of Polarity　Opposing seeds like 水 and 火 cancel each other, requiring aharmonizing catalyst.
  • R3 Law of the Latent School　A medium catalyst writes a third School into the spell.
  • R4 Law of Fourfold Overload　Materials spanning four Schools overload, requiring a Concept Schoolmedium。
  • R5 Law of the Vessel　Lv7 and above require avessel catalyst to carry it.
  • R6 Law of the Anchor　Every ritual needs at least one seed character or pure stack as an anchor.
  • R7 Law of School Exclusion　When two mutually exclusive Schools appear together, a Space Schoolmedium。
  • R8 Law of Purity　Stacking one seed too many times locks it, requiring abreaker catalyst.

The laws are gates, not punishments. The demo lets you play R2／R3／R6／R7; the remaining slots are reserved — R1 at a wide rank gap and the dedicated catalysts of R4／R5／R8 open up in the full version.

4. The four catalysts

The purpleritual catalyst slottakes one seed character and resolves the laws above:

harmonizingresolves R2, polarity opposition
vesselresolves R5, higher-rank overflow
mediumresolves R4／R7, and writes the latent School
breakerresolves R8, purity lock

One seed can hold several roles. 「龠」 qualifies at once as vessel, breaker, harmonizer and concept medium, the only origin that can resolve multiple constraints on its own.

5. Same base, different character — the game’s most distinctive mechanic

The bluetuning catalyst slotdoes not resolve laws; it does something else: the same materials with a different tuning catalyst produce an entirely different character.

木×4＝𣛧 the base character with no tuning
木×4＋糸＝𱤏 with the tuning catalyst 「糸」

The demo lets you complete 14 of these branches, and the full version has 442. Tuning routes are never handed to you — you may spend 1Inspiration to read one, or work it out yourself.

6. Where Inspiration comes from

Inspiration is a limited research resource, spent on reading tuning routes or buying hints. Discovering a character grants no Inspiration by itself — it comes from three places:

  • UnlockAchievements(each stage +1）
  • ClaimOrigin Quest(each stage +1）
  • Research Supplyquests (as marked on the card)

With zero Inspiration you can still synthesise freely — you simply have no assistance.

7. The main line: awakening the forty-nine origins

You begin with seven seeds; the other 42 are woken one by one throughOrigin Quest. Every stage has a clear condition (discover a named character, reach a compendium count, resolve a law⋯⋯）， and once complete, youClaim by Hand. The main line runs 54 stages.

The main line is built from 15 chapters, 700 core canonical characters and the origin ／law trials. After the ending you can still complete 350 optional canonical characters, gathering all 1,050 canonical characters; the remaining 1,842 belong to research in the Beyond.

8. Achievements and collection

The demo includes 82 attainable achievements; the full version adds Inscriptions Beyond and long-term collection goals. Some achievementsevolve with your progress — “Pilgrimage of Ranks”, for instance, grows from its first ascent all the way to the Beyond.

Controls

  • Clicka card into the first empty slot; you can alsoDrag。
  • Right-click any card for its full details: composition, School, reading and spell effect.
  • The left panel supportsSearch — type a character, a component, pinyin or bopomofo. Entering 「口木」 finds characters containing both components.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move through the card grid,Enter to place,Esc to close a window.

You need memorise nothing. When something is unclear, right-click it; when a law blocks you, read the red line. The game teaches itself.

Hanzi Grimoire is built on many public datasets and freely licensed resources. They are listed below by licence requirement and degree of contribution.

  • Jigmo（2025-09-12 ) — a Mincho family covering the CJK extension blocks. All 2,941 card glyphs are embedded offline and do not depend on the player’s system fonts. Licence CC0 1.0。 Official Page

Language Data

  • Unicode Unihan Database — source of Hanyu Pinyin (kMandarin and related fields). Bopomofo is converted from pinyin by deterministic rules. Licence Unicode License。 Official Page
  • Unicode IDS Data — the basis for decomposing characters, used to derive the composition of 2,892 characters.

Technology

  • The game is a single HTML file — no framework, no external dependencies, playable offline.
  • The data generation and validation pipeline is written in Python 3.12 .
  • Cloud saves are optional and use Firebase; without signing in everything stays local and nothing is sent.

Special Notice

Every character in the game either genuinely exists or is structurally valid under the IDS rules — none are invented. The spell descriptions are original to this work; 517 have been edited by hand and the rest are generated drafts, still being revised.

If you find any error in a glyph, reading or dataset, open an issue or email pimail307@gmail.com — it is much appreciated.
